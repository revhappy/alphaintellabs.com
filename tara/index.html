One birth moment, three charts

Three traditions that disagree, held side by side.

Western, Vedic and Chinese astrology measure different things from the same instant, and they do not always say the same thing about it. Tara keeps all three and names the disagreement instead of averaging it away.

Western

Tropical

Measured from the equinox. The chart most people have seen.

  • Sun, Moon and Ascendant to the degree
  • Mercury through Pluto, each with sign and degree
  • All 45 aspect pairs, each with its exact orb
  • Element and modality weight across every placement

Vedic

Sidereal · Jyotisha

Measured from the fixed stars, which puts most planets a sign back.

  • Lagna and Rashi from your own ayanamsa
  • Nakshatra with pada and ruling planet
  • Vimshottari mahadasha, and the years left in it
  • Every body converted, not just the Moon

Bazi · Four Pillars

  • Month set by solar term, year by Li Chun

The engine

What comes out of your birth moment.

Every line below is computed on our servers from your date, time and coordinates. Nothing here is a lookup table keyed to your sun sign, and the reading you get is written from these numbers.

Ten bodiesSun → Pluto
Geocentric ecliptic longitude for the exact minute you were born, resolved to the arcsecond and carried at four decimal places of a degree through everything downstream.
The AscendantRising sign
Derived from local sidereal time and the obliquity of the ecliptic at your coordinates. It advances one degree every four minutes, which is why Tara asks for the minute and not the morning.
45 aspect pairsNatal geometry
Every pair of bodies tested for conjunction, opposition, trine, square and sextile — 6° orb, 4° on the sextile — and each hit is kept with its measured orb rather than a label.
Element weightAgainst the mean
Fire, Earth, Air and Water counted across all ten placements, then compared against the mean. A chart with no real dominance comes back balanced instead of being handed a winner it did not earn.
Lahiri ayanamsaChitrapaksha
Precession and the principal nutation term, evaluated for your birth date rather than taken as a constant. Every tropical position is then carried into the sidereal chart with it.
27 nakshatrasWith pada and lord
The Moon's exact place inside a 13°20′ lunar mansion, down to which quarter of it — one of four padas — and the planet that rules the mansion.
Vimshottari dasha120-year cycle
The cycle is started from how far the Moon had already travelled into its nakshatra at birth, then run forward to today: your current mahadasha and the years remaining in it.
Four PillarsBazi
The month boundary is the true solar term, found by searching the Sun's longitude to a billionth of a radian, and the year turns at Li Chun — so a February birthday lands in the right animal instead of the calendar's.
Historical timezoneAt your birthplace
Your coordinates resolve to the zone that place kept on that date, with the offset in force at the time applied before any of the above runs. Wartime clocks and repealed daylight rules come out right.
Live transitsEvery reading
The current sky is recomputed for each reading, and its aspects to your natal chart are measured and ranked by exactness before anything is written — so the tightest contact leads, not the best-known planet.

How it writes

It tells you when the systems disagree.

Where the three traditions point the same way, Tara says so and leans in. Where they contradict each other, it names the contradiction rather than splitting the difference. A reading with no difficulty in it is not an honest reading, and health, legal and money questions stay at the level of tendency and timing.

Before you start

Every reading in Tara is written by AI and is for entertainment. The astronomy underneath it is computed, but the interpretation on top is generated, and it can be wrong, incomplete, or contradict itself between readings.

Nothing in the app is medical, legal, financial or psychological advice. Do not use it to decide whether to take a medicine, sign a contract, move money, or leave a relationship — talk to someone qualified in that field. Tara will not diagnose, and it does not predict illness, death, pregnancy or legal outcomes.

Readings need an accurate birth time to be worth anything. A guessed hour moves your Ascendant by fifteen degrees and can change your Vedic Lagna, your Moon's pada and your Bazi hour pillar with it. Full terms are in the Terms of Service.
