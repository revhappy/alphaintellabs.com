Support

Need help with Tara? Email alphaintellabs@gmail.com and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please include the email you sign in with and, if it is about a reading, the date it was written.

What is Tara?

Tara computes your Western (tropical), Vedic (sidereal) and Chinese (Bazi) charts from your birth date, time and place, and uses AI to write readings and chat replies from them. Tara is for entertainment only. Astrology is not a science, readings are not predictions of fact, and nothing in the app is medical, legal or financial advice.

What is free, and what is Tara Pro?

The daily reading is free, with a small daily allowance of messages to Tara. Tara Pro adds up to 100 messages a day, compatibility readings, weekly Deep Dive readings and the interactive Chart Insights view.

In the iPhone and iPad app, Tara Pro is an auto-renewing subscription: monthly or annual. New subscribers can start with a 1-week free trial. Prices are shown in the app in your local currency before you buy.

How do I restore a purchase?

Sign in with the same account you used before, then tap Restore Purchases on the Tara Pro screen or in Settings. Use the same Apple ID (or Google account on Android) that made the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Deleting the app or your Tara account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your store settings to stop future charges:

  • iPhone or iPad: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Or in Tara, go to Settings → Manage Subscription. You can also visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How is AI used, and can I turn it off?

Readings and chat replies are written by an AI model (OpenAI) from your computed chart and your messages. The app asks for your consent before anything is sent. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → AI Readings; the AI features lock again until you agree. If a reply looks wrong or inappropriate, tap Report under it.

I don't know my birth time. Can I still use Tara?

Yes. Choose the unknown-time option when you enter your details. Tara will still compute your chart, but your Rising sign, houses and hour pillar depend on the exact time and will be less reliable. You can edit your birth details later in your profile.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete Account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. Step-by-step detail, including what is deleted, is on the account deletion page.
