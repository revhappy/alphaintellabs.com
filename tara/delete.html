Delete Your Account

In the app

Open Tara, go to Settings → Danger Zone and tap Delete Account. If you signed in with Apple, you will be asked to confirm with Apple once more so we can revoke Tara's access to your Apple ID. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email alphaintellabs@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Tara account”. We will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and account record
  • Your birth details, name and generated astrology profile
  • Your readings: daily readings, compatibility reports (including the other person's birth details), Deep Dives and chart analysis
  • Your conversations with Tara
  • Any share links you created, which stop working
  • Reports you filed about AI replies

What is kept

  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google, and by our purchase-management provider, as billing and tax rules require.
  • A guest account that was never signed in exists only on your device. Deleting it in Settings, or deleting the app, removes access to it.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple or Google Play subscription settings to stop future charges. See how to cancel.
