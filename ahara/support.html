Frequently asked questions

How do scans work?

Open Ahara, point the camera at a product ingredient label — or choose a photo of one — and tap the shutter. Ahara reads the label and shows ingredient information. If you optionally set your own goals, each scan can also show how the label compares with those goals.

How many free trial scans do I get?

You can try one free scan without creating an account. After you sign in, you get five more free scans (six in total, including the first one). These free scans are a one-time allowance, not a monthly reset. When they are used up, you can buy a scan pack or subscribe for a monthly allowance.

What is the difference between scan packs and subscriptions?

Scan packs are one-time purchases that add a set number of scans you can use when you need them: 10 scans ($0.99), 60 scans ($4.99), or 150 scans ($9.99).

Subscriptions give you a monthly scan allowance that renews with your plan: Basic (80 scans per month), Pro (200), or Max (500). Each tier is available monthly or annually. Annual plans include a one-week free trial, then bill yearly.

You can use a scan pack on its own, or alongside a subscription if you need extra scans in a given period.

How do I restore purchases?

On the plans screen or in Settings, tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Restoring applies to subscriptions and scan packs already purchased on that store account.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Deleting the app or your Ahara account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your store settings to stop future charges:

  • iPhone or iPad: open the Settings app, tap your name, then tap Subscriptions. Or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: open the Google Play Store, tap your profile, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete Account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone. You can also email alphaintellabs@gmail.com from the address on your account. Step-by-step detail, including what is deleted and what is kept, is on the account deletion page.

Where is the privacy policy?

Read the Ahara Privacy Policy. It explains what we collect, how scans and goals are handled, and how to withdraw consent.

Where does product data come from?

Ahara reads the ingredient list in the photo you take. If a barcode is in view, we may also look up that number in Open Food Facts or Open Beauty Facts, open product databases used under the Open Database License. Those entries can be incomplete or out of date. The pack in your hand is the source of truth.

Ahara provides ingredient information and label transparency. It does not diagnose conditions or give medical or dietary advice.
