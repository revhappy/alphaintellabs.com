Delete Your Account

In the app

Open Ahara, go to Settings, and tap Delete Account. Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

Without the app

Email alphaintellabs@gmail.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my Ahara account”. We will confirm once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in and account record
  • Your health and dietary goals stored in your account
  • Your saved favorites, including their chat conversations

Scan history stored on your device is removed when you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app. Share links expire after 90 days, or email us a link to remove it sooner.

What is kept

  • A one-way hash of your email and the number of free scans used, to prevent free-trial abuse. It cannot be reversed to reveal your email.
  • The device record: device identifier, scan counts, and any purchases made without an account. This stops deleting and reinstalling from resetting the free trial. Its link to your account is removed.
  • Rate-limit counters: request timestamps and a daily count, stored under your internal account ID. They contain no email, goals or scan content.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and our purchase-management provider, as billing and tax rules require.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your Apple or Google Play subscription settings to stop future charges.
