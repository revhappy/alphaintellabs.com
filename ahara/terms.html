1. Description of Service

Ahara is an AI-powered product label analyzer. You photograph product labels (food, beverages, supplements, skincare and cosmetics, toothpaste, medicine, and other consumer products), and the App uses artificial intelligence — specifically, large language models operated by third-party service providers — to read the label text and provide informational analysis of the ingredients, including quality scores, potential allergens, ingredient interactions, comparison between products, follow-up chat, and general commentary. If you choose to record personal goals, the App also returns a “Goal Fit” assessment of how well a product suits them; see Section 5.
