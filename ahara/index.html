How it works

Three taps, standing in the aisle.

No barcode database to miss your product, no typing. Ahara reads the label in front of you.

Point and shoot

Open the camera, frame the ingredient panel, tap the shutter. Long labels can be captured across up to four pages.

Watch it come together

Results stream in as they're written — scores first, then each ingredient, one at a time. No blank loading screen.

The part that's about you

Every scan, measured against your goals.

A product that's fine for most people can be exactly wrong for you. Tell Ahara what you're managing — once — and every analysis from then on opens with a Goal Fit verdict: a 0–100 score, a plain-language explanation, and named chips for whatever conflicts.

  • 74 goals to pick from, across five kindsHow you eat · what you're working toward · ingredients to avoid · allergies & intolerances · skincare and personal care.
  • Real conditions, not just dietsPCOS, type 2 diabetes, thyroid, gout, kidney, migraine triggers, coeliac, pregnancy and nursing, fertility. Plus 300 characters in your own words.
  • It tells you why, not just yes or noConflicts are named and explained against the specific ingredient that caused them, so you can judge the trade-off yourself.
  • Yours, and only yoursGoals stay on your device unless you sign in to sync them, and they are stripped from anything you share. Clear them all in one tap.

Goal Fit is informational, not clinical. A product shown as fitting your goals has not been reviewed by a healthcare professional, and selecting an allergen goal does not make allergen detection reliable — always read the physical label.

Privacy

Your health goals never leave with the share link.

Scan history, photos, and chats stay on your device by default. Label images are analyzed in real time and discarded — never kept on our servers. Your goals are optional, stay on-device unless you sign in to sync them, are stripped from anything you share, and clear in one tap. We don't sell your data, don't run ads, and don't use your data to train AI models. Read the full privacy policy.

Important — please read

Every analysis in Ahara, including Goal Fit, is generated by AI and can be wrong. Labels may be misread, ingredients misidentified, and scores, warnings, and commentary may be incomplete, outdated, or simply incorrect.

Ahara is an informational and educational tool. It is not medical advice, nutritional counselling, or a diagnosis, and it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. A product shown as fitting your goals has not been assessed by a clinician. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional, registered dietitian, or allergist before making decisions about your health, diet, medication, or the products you use.

If you have a food allergy, do not rely on this app. Allergen detection is not reliable and can miss hidden forms of common allergens — and setting an allergen goal does not change that. Always read the physical label and check with the manufacturer.
