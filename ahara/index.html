How it works

Three taps, standing in the aisle.

No barcode database to miss your product, no typing. Ahara reads the label in front of you.

Point and shoot

Open the camera, frame the ingredient panel, tap the shutter. Long labels can be captured across up to four pages.

Watch it come together

Results stream in as they're written — scores first, then each ingredient, one at a time. No blank loading screen.

Decide with context

Tell Ahara what you're working toward once, and every scan afterwards is judged against it, not against a generic average.

What you get

The label, translated.

Everything the packaging is technically telling you, in language you can act on.

Scores you can act on

An overall quality score, a safety score, and a health-impact score — each one traced back to the ingredients that moved it.

Every ingredient, explained

What it is, why it's in there, and how it's regarded — plus processing classification, glycemic impact, and comedogenic ratings where they apply.

Hidden things, surfaced

Sugars under twelve different names, hidden salts, trans fats, and allergens in forms the front of the box never mentions.

Built around your goals

Pick from dietary patterns, allergies, ingredients to avoid, and health circumstances. Each scan is then rated on how well it fits you.

Compare up to four

Two shelf options that look identical? Scan them side by side and see exactly where they diverge, ingredient by ingredient.

Ask follow-up questions

"Is this okay while pregnant?" "What's a cleaner alternative?" Chat about any scan and get answers grounded in that specific label.

Inside the app

Calm by design.

No red alarm bells, no engagement bait. Just the label, read carefully — in light or dark.

Privacy

Your scans stay on your phone.

Scan history, photos, and chats are stored on your device by default. Label images are analyzed in real time and discarded — they are never kept on our servers. Health goals are optional, never appear in anything you share, and can be cleared in one tap. We don't sell your data, don't run ads, and don't use your data to train AI models. Read the full privacy policy.

Important — please read

Every analysis in Ahara is generated by AI and can be wrong. Labels may be misread, ingredients misidentified, and scores, warnings, and commentary may be incomplete, outdated, or simply incorrect.

Ahara is an informational and educational tool. It is not medical advice, nutritional counselling, or a diagnosis, and it is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional, registered dietitian, or allergist before making decisions about your health, diet, medication, or the products you use.

If you have a food allergy, do not rely on this app. Allergen detection is not reliable and can miss hidden forms of common allergens. Always read the physical label and check with the manufacturer.
