2. Health and Safety

Read this section before using the app. It is the most important part of these terms.

Seizure disorders

If you have epilepsy, a history of seizures, or any photosensitive or audiogenic seizure disorder, consult a qualified doctor before using Aum. Every session in the app includes rhythmic auditory stimulation, and some include a pulsing visual guide. Rhythmic sensory stimulation is a recognised seizure trigger in susceptible individuals. Stop using the app immediately and seek medical attention if you experience dizziness, altered vision, eye or muscle twitching, disorientation, involuntary movement, or loss of awareness.

Do not use while driving

Do not use Aum while driving, cycling, operating machinery, or doing anything else where reduced alertness could cause harm. The sleep and deep-rest programs are specifically designed to induce drowsiness and they are effective.

Talk to a clinician first if

  • you are pregnant;
  • you have a pacemaker, cochlear implant, or other implanted or wearable medical device;
  • you have a heart condition, or a neurological or psychiatric condition — including but not limited to arrhythmia, migraine with aura, tinnitus, vertigo, PTSD or a dissociative disorder;
  • you are being treated for any condition that could be affected by relaxation, altered breathing or altered alertness.

Breathing exercises

The breathing patterns in Aum involve slowed breathing and, in some patterns, breath holds. Never practise breath holding in or near water. Stop immediately if you feel light-headed, dizzy, short of breath, or anxious, and return to normal breathing. If you have asthma, COPD, or any other respiratory condition, speak to your doctor before using paced breathing.

Hearing

Prolonged listening at high volume can permanently damage your hearing, regardless of the frequency being played. Use the lowest volume at which the session is comfortable. Aum recommends headphones for the binaural layer; this makes volume discipline more important, not less.

Stop if it doesn't feel right

Discontinue use if you experience headache, nausea, ear discomfort, anxiety, agitation or any other adverse effect, and consult a healthcare professional if it persists.

3. Claims, Evidence and Tradition

Aum draws on the Solfeggio frequency tradition and on related tuning traditions. The therapeutic claims attached to those frequencies by tradition are not supported by clinical evidence, and we do not make them. The app quotes those claims where they are historically relevant, attributes them, and corrects them in the same place — for example, 528 Hz is widely described as repairing DNA; that claim originates in a 1990s book, not in any laboratory result.

The app labels each of its layers with an evidence tier — researched, emerging or traditional — and shows that labelling to you in a dedicated screen. Those tiers describe the general state of published research into a practice, not a prediction about you. Slow paced breathing at around six breaths per minute has the best supporting evidence of anything in the app; the specific frequencies have none. No outcome is promised, and individual responses vary widely.
