What it is

A session combines a tone, a rhythm and a visual breathing guide. Sound and on-screen cues follow the selected breathing pattern.

Choose a preset breathing pattern or set your own timing. Breathe comfortably, skip holds if needed, and stop if you feel unwell.

Nothing here is a recording. Every sound is made on your phone as it plays, rather than streamed from a fixed recording. Nobody talks over it, and it runs with your phone in airplane mode.

The feel of it

Sound with optional vibration.

Optional haptics pulse your phone alongside the sound. Choose the vibration strength in settings; availability depends on your device.

  • Layered tonesLower octaves add depth to the selected tone.
  • Timed phone vibrationThe vibration motor pulses on the same rhythm as the sound, so the beat arrives through your hands as well as your ears.
  • Stereo binaural optionA slow beat that isn't in either ear on its own — it appears from the difference between them.

Breath

Choose your breathing pace.

Pick a pattern and the whole session runs at that rate — the swell of the tone, the pulse in your phone, the orb on screen.

About the sounds

Choose by listening.

Tone frequencies are sound settings, not treatments. Traditional names and research discussed in the app do not establish that Aum produces a particular health or cognitive result. No outcome is promised; choose sounds you find comfortable.

Privacy

Your practice history stays on your device.

There is no account to make and no sign-in. Your session history is written to your own device and never leaves your device, and one tap clears it. We run no analytics and sell no ads, so we hold no record of you at all. Buying, restoring and checking purchase access use a connection. Read the full privacy policy.

Important

Aum plays sound and vibration. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

If you have epilepsy or a seizure disorder, talk to a doctor first. Every session uses rhythmic stimulation.

Don't use it while driving or operating machinery. Check with a clinician first if you're pregnant, have a pacemaker, or are being treated for a heart, neurological or psychiatric condition. Keep the volume low.
