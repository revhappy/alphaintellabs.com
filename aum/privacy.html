Aum

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy explains what Aum (“the app”, “we”, “us”) does with your information. Aum is published by Alpha Intel Labs.

Summary

  • We have no server and no account. There is nothing to sign into, and we hold no record that identifies you.
  • The app does not send what you do in it anywhere. Your sessions, settings and history are written to your own device.
  • One third party, and only for purchases. RevenueCat checks whether you have bought the full version. It receives an anonymous, randomly generated installation identifier, your store receipt if you buy, and the basic technical details that every internet connection carries. It never receives your name, your email, or anything about how you use the app.
  • All audio is synthesised on your phone. There is no streaming, so there is no listening record to keep — yours or ours.
  • The app works fully in airplane mode, apart from buying or restoring the full version.
  • There are no ads, no trackers, and no analytics, attribution or advertising SDKs, and we do not sell your data.
  • Deleting the app deletes what it stored on your device. Your phone's own backup can keep a copy (see section 7).

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask for personal details. Aum does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it has no facility to receive them. There is no account to create and no profile to fill in.

We do not operate a server, and the app never sends anything to us. We do not run analytics, crash reporting, attribution, advertising or A/B testing SDKs of any kind.

The exception is the purchase check in sections 3 and 4. RevenueCat does this check for us. The first time the app connects, RevenueCat makes an anonymous record for this installation. That record contains a random identifier and, if you buy, your store receipt. It does not include your name or email address.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

Aum saves a small amount of data locally, in your device's app storage, so that it can work the way you left it. The app does not send this data to us or to RevenueCat, and we cannot read it.

Your phone's own backup can include this data. On Android, this is the backup to your Google account. On iPhone, it is an iCloud backup or a backup to a computer. That backup belongs to your account, not to us. When you restore a phone from a backup, or install Aum again on a phone with backup on, the backup can put this data back.

Session history

When you finish a session of at least one minute, Aum records its name, its type, how long you actually listened, the breathing pattern in use, whether it ran to completion, and the time it happened. This is what draws the “Your practice” screen. It is capped at the most recent 500 entries. To erase all of it, tap “Clear history” on the “Your practice” screen, then tap it again to confirm.

Your settings

Volume, theme, Presence step, pulse and vibration strength, and your chosen breathing pattern, ambience and bell — including any per-program preferences and any custom breathing pattern you build.

Purchase state

A single flag recording whether the full version has been unlocked on this device. It contains no purchase details, no payment information and no identifier.

Trial state

A count of how many of your five trial sessions you have used, and the date the trial started. To establish that date, the app reads the installation date your operating system already records for it. That date is read on your device, is used only to work out how long the trial has left, and is never transmitted — to us or to anyone else.

While a trial session is playing, the app also notes what is playing, how long it has run and how much of it has been counted, so that a session interrupted by the app closing is still counted for the time it played. That note is deleted as soon as the session ends. Like everything else in this section, it stays on your device.

3. Network Access

Aum contains no streamed or downloaded content. Every tone, pulse, bell and soundscape is generated by the app itself while it plays, and the entire catalogue is compiled into the app binary. No audio content is downloaded.

The Android build declares the standard INTERNET permission, which the purchase flow requires. All network use in Aum is for purchases, and it goes through RevenueCat's software:

  • When the app starts, it asks RevenueCat whether this installation has the full version, and it gets the current price from the store.
  • Each time you return to the app, RevenueCat's software can check again.
  • When you buy or restore, the app sends the purchase to RevenueCat to verify it.

Nothing else in Aum uses the network, and the app does not send data to our own servers. When your device is offline, these checks fail without a message and the app continues to work. Only buying and restoring need a connection.

4. Purchases

The full version of Aum is a one-time purchase. Payment is handled entirely by the app store you downloaded it from — Google Play, or the Apple App Store if and when an iOS version is released. We never see or receive your payment details. The store tells the app only whether a valid purchase exists.

Your purchase is subject to that store's own privacy policy and terms, which we do not control.

Aum uses RevenueCat to verify entitlements — that is, to establish whether this installation has paid, including when you restore a purchase on a new device. To do that, RevenueCat receives an anonymous, randomly generated installation identifier that it creates itself, together with the receipt the app store issues. Like any internet service, RevenueCat also sees the IP address that each request comes from. Its software sends basic technical details: the platform, the system and app versions, and your locale and currency. It does not receive your name, your email address, your device's advertising identifier, or anything about which sessions you play or when. We do not send it anything else.

RevenueCat keeps this record on its servers for us. Deleting the app does not delete it. See section 7 to ask us to delete it. RevenueCat's handling of this data is covered by RevenueCat's own privacy policy.

5. Permissions

Aum requests only what it needs to play a session, and explains each one here:

  • Vibration — drives the haptic layer, which pulses in time with your breath.
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock — keep audio running with the screen off. Without these, Android suspends the app partway through and a 90-minute sleep program dies in the middle of the night.
  • Notifications — shows the standard playback notification while a session is running, so you can see and stop it from the lock screen.
  • Internet — as described in section 3.

Aum does not request the microphone, camera, location, contacts, calendar, phone state, or access to your files or photos. It cannot record audio.

6. Children's Privacy

Aum is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — of any age — because the app does not ask for it. Parents should be aware of the health and safety warnings in our Terms of Service, particularly the one concerning seizure disorders, before allowing a child to use rhythmic audio stimulation.

7. Your Rights and Choices

We do not keep a profile of you, so there is no profile to opt out of. The data that Aum stores on your device is under your control:

  • To erase your practice history, tap “Clear history” on the “Your practice” screen.
  • On Android, clear the app's storage in your device settings to erase all the data Aum has written to your device. On iPhone, delete the app to do this. If you have bought the full version, “Restore” on the unlock screen gets your purchase back.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data it stored on your device. A copy can stay in your phone's own backup (see section 2) until that backup is replaced or deleted. To remove that copy, delete it in your Google account or iCloud backup settings.

RevenueCat keeps the anonymous record in section 4. It contains no name or email, so we cannot find it from your name or email. To ask us to delete it, email us the order number from your store receipt. We will find the record with that number and ask RevenueCat to delete it.

The app store also keeps a record of your purchase. Requests about that record, including refunds, go to the store.

8. Data Security

Aum has no account and we do not run our own server for the app. The only data off your device is the anonymous purchase record in section 4. Data written on your device is protected by your device's own operating-system sandbox and whatever screen lock and encryption you have enabled on it.

9. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page and publish the revised version here. If Aum’s data practices change, we will update this policy.
