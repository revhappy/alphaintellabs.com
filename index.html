Aum

Aum plays a steady tone and paces your breathing with a light on screen — seven kinds of session, for getting to sleep, coming down after a bad day, or holding focus while you work. It labels the evidence behind every part of itself: slow breathing is well researched, rhythmic sound is thinner than most apps let on, and 528 Hz does not repair your DNA. Every sound is made on the phone as it plays, so nothing loops and none of it needs a signal.

PromptCAD

Type the part you want in plain English — “a 60 × 40 × 10 mm mounting plate with a 12 mm hole through the centre and 3 mm fillets” — and PromptCAD builds it in FreeCAD as a real feature tree: a box, a cylinder, a cut, a fillet, every one a node you can open and edit like something you'd drawn by hand. It measures the solid it just built and checks it against the numbers you asked for, so you find out about a wrong part instead of taking one home. Exports STEP and STL, runs on your own PC against a local model, and sends nothing anywhere.
